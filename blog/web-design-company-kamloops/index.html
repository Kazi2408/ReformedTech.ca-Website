Choosing a web design company is a big decision for a small business. Your website is often the first impression a customer gets, and it’s a meaningful investment. This guide covers what to look for in Kamloops, what it should cost in 2026, the red flags to avoid and the questions to ask before you sign anything.

What “best” really means for a Kamloops business

The best web design company for you is the one that can show how your website will bring in enquiries, not the one with the flashiest portfolio. For most Kamloops service businesses, a good website should:

  • Make it obvious what you do, where you do it and how to contact you, within seconds.
  • Work well on a phone, where most local searches happen.
  • Load quickly.
  • Be built so Google can understand and rank it, with local SEO considered from day one.
  • Be easy for you to update and fully owned by you.

7 things to look for in a web design company

  1. A focus on leads, not just looks. They ask about your customers, services and goals before they talk about colours and fonts.
  2. Local SEO included. Page titles, service pages, location content, schema and Google Business Profile alignment are part of the build, not an expensive add-on.
  3. Mobile-first design. They design and test for phones first, then scale up to desktop.
  4. Relevant examples. They can show sites for businesses similar to yours and explain what those sites were built to achieve.
  5. Clear, written pricing. You know what’s included, what costs extra and what ongoing fees to expect.
  6. You own everything. Your domain, hosting account, website files and content are in your name.
  7. Support after launch. There’s a clear plan for updates, fixes and reporting once the site is live.

How much does web design cost in Kamloops in 2026?

Prices vary with scope, but these ranges are typical for a professional build from a local agency:

Type of siteTypical price (CAD)Usually suits
Starter website$699–$1,500New or small businesses that need a professional, mobile-friendly presence with a few core pages
Business growth website$1,500–$3,500Established service businesses that need individual service pages, SEO and booking or lead capture
Enterprise or e-commerce$3,500–$8,000+Online stores, multi-location businesses and sites with custom features or integrations

The main things that move the price are the number of pages, how much copywriting you need, e-commerce or booking features, integrations with your other tools, and how much SEO work is included.

Watch the ongoing costs, not just the build price. Ask what hosting, maintenance, plugin licences and support will cost each month, and whether you can leave without penalty.

Red flags to avoid

  • They register your domain in their name. If the relationship ends, you could lose your web address.
  • Long lock-in contracts with steep cancellation fees.
  • No plan for SEO, or SEO described only as “we’ll add keywords”.
  • A template sold as “custom” with no explanation of what’s actually being built for you.
  • Guaranteed #1 rankings. No one can honestly guarantee a specific Google position.
  • Vague quotes with no list of pages, features or revisions.
  • A slow or broken website of their own on mobile.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. Who will own the domain, hosting and website files when the project is finished?
  2. What’s included in the price, and what would cost extra?
  3. How will the site be set up to rank for my services in Kamloops?
  4. Can I update the content myself? Will you train me?
  5. How long will it take, and what do you need from me?
  6. What happens after launch: support, updates and reporting?
  7. Can you show me a site you built for a business like mine, and what it was designed to achieve?

Local agency or remote agency?

There are good web designers everywhere, and a remote team can absolutely do great work. A Kamloops-based agency has a few practical advantages: it already understands the local market and nearby communities, it can meet in person when that’s helpful, and it works in your time zone. Whatever you choose, judge them on the criteria above, not on location alone.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a small business website cost in Kamloops?
A professional starter site typically costs $699–$1,500 CAD. A business growth site with SEO and booking or lead capture is usually $1,500–$3,500, and e-commerce or custom builds start around $3,500.
How long does it take to build a website?
It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback are ready. Ask for a written timeline with milestones before you start.
Should I use a website builder instead of hiring a designer?
A DIY builder can work for a very simple site if you have the time. Hiring a designer makes sense when your website needs to bring in leads, rank locally and look professional without taking up your own hours.
Do I own my website after it’s built?
